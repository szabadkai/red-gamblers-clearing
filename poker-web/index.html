Field guide

On the trail

Defeat the foes at each briar gate. Gather three embers and reach the lantern to face the guardian.

Lantern posts mark fall recovery: falling returns you to the last lit post. Open briar gates save your trail progress for death, retry, and your next visit. At a locked gate, a brief arrow points toward a remaining foe, including whether it is above or below you.

Each clearing has an optional challenge for a heart and a card drop. Follow its gold markers or use E, D-pad Up, or the nearby action button. Completing it never blocks your journey.

Before every guardian, keep your prepared hand and draw meter or deal five fresh cards. Your chosen opening is restored when you retry that encounter.

Playing your hand

Play the highlighted cards together, or cycle to save a pair for later. Throw a single card for 6 damage. Flushes and higher awaken Dark Red for six seconds.

Every third landed sword swing earns a card drop. Stand near it and press E, D-pad Up, or Collect to choose between two cards. Combat pauses only when you collect. With a full hand, choose a slot to replace or play a combination to make room. Esc or B leaves the drop for later.

Pairs hit hard, two pair explodes, and trips and straights pierce. Spades pierce, diamonds fill the draw meter, clubs stagger ordinary foes, and hearts heal. Against guardians, clubs lengthen a recovery opening once, and piercing shots cut roots, silk, rings, and thrown axes in their path. Every guardian can still be beaten with the sword.
